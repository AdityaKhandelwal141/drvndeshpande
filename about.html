✦ About Dr. Deshpande

40+ Years of Trusted Orthopedic Excellence

Restoring Pain-Free Movement

Born on 28 February 1958, Dr. V. N. Deshpande completed his M.S. Orthopedics from G.M.C., Bombay. He is a highly respected orthopedic surgeon with a distinguished academic and clinical career.

He has served as Registrar at G.T. Hospital, Mumbai, as a Postgraduate Teacher for DNB, and held the position of Head, Department of Orthopedic Surgery.
