Common Spine Problems

Conditions We Treat

Accurate diagnosis is the first step to effective treatment. We treat a wide range of spinal conditions.

⚡

Herniated Disc (Slipped Disc)

When the cushion between vertebrae bulges out, pressing on nerves and causing pain, numbness, or weakness in the leg.

🔻

Sciatica

Pain that radiates along the path of the sciatic nerve, branching from your lower back through your hips and buttocks and down each leg.

📏

Spinal Stenosis

Narrowing of the spaces within your spine, which can put pressure on the nerves, causing pain and cramping in the legs.

🦴

Cervical Spondylosis

Age-related wear and tear affecting the spinal disks in your neck, leading to stiffness and neck pain.

Treatment Approaches

We Prioritize Non-Surgical Care

Over 90% of back pain cases improve without surgery. Our approach focuses on conservative management first, utilizing surgery only when absolutely necessary.

  • ✓ Targeted Physiotherapy & Core Strengthening
  • ✓ Medication & Pain Management
  • ✓ Epidural Steroid Injections
  • ✓ Lifestyle & Ergonomic Modifications
🩺

When is Surgery Needed?

Surgery is considered if you have severe nerve compression, loss of bowel/bladder control (medical emergency), or if pain doesn't improve after 6-12 weeks of conservative care.

Protect Your Spine Daily

Healthy Habits & Ergonomics

Small changes in your daily routine can prevent back pain and keep your spine healthy.

✅ Do's

  • 🪑 Sit with support: Use a chair with good lower back support and keep your feet flat on the floor.
  • 🏋️ Lift correctly: Bend at your knees, not your waist, and keep the object close to your body.
  • 🚶 Stay active: Regular walking and core exercises (like planks) strengthen your back.
  • 🛏️ Sleep smart: Sleep on your side with a pillow between your knees to keep your spine aligned.

❌ Don'ts

  • 📱 Avoid 'Text Neck': Don't look down at your phone for long periods. Bring the screen up to eye level.
  • 🛋️ Don't slouch: Avoid sinking into soft sofas for hours, which strains the lower back.
  • 📦 Don't twist while lifting: Never lift heavy objects and twist your torso at the same time.
  • 🕰️ Don't sit too long: Avoid sitting continuously for more than 45 minutes. Take a short walk to stretch.
