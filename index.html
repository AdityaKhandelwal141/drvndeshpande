Knee Replacement

Understand Every Step

Overview

Total knee replacement is a surgical procedure in which a damaged or arthritic knee joint is replaced with an artificial implant. It helps relieve chronic knee pain, correct deformity, and restore smooth knee movement, allowing patients to return to daily activities with ease.

Common Symptoms

  • Persistent knee pain that limits daily activities
  • Stiffness and reduced range of motion
  • Swelling and inflammation
  • Knee deformity
  • Pain even at rest or at night

When Is It Needed?

Surgery is considered when non-surgical treatments — medication, physiotherapy, injections — no longer provide adequate relief and pain significantly affects quality of life.

The Procedure

The damaged cartilage and bone are removed and replaced with metal and plastic components. Modern techniques focus on preserving soft tissue and restoring alignment.

Recovery

Most patients begin walking with assistance within a day. Physiotherapy is essential. Full recovery typically takes several weeks to months.

Benefits

  • Significant pain relief
  • Improved mobility and function
  • Correction of deformity
  • Better quality of life

FAQs

How long does an implant last? Modern implants can last 15–20 years or more with proper care.
